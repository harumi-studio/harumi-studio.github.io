  • 使う目的：お返事、不具合の調査、ご要望の検討（アプリの改善）のためだけに使います。
  • ほかへの提供：法令に基づく場合を除き、ほかの人や会社には渡しません。メールは Google LLC の Gmail で受け取り・保管しています（国外のサーバーに置かれることがあります）。
  • 保存期間：やり取りが終わってから 2 年を目安に削除します。早めの削除をご希望の場合は、このアドレスにご連絡ください。
  • 当方の氏名・住所、開示・訂正・削除などのご請求の手続き、安全のための取り組みは、ご請求があれば遅滞なくお知らせします。苦情・ご相談も同じアドレスで受け付けます。
  • 画面の写真を添付するときは、ほかの方の情報や見られたくない内容が写っていないかご確認ください。

Support

For questions, bug reports or requests about Harumi Studio apps, please email us.

Contact

harumi.studio.jp@gmail.com

We read every message and reply in order. A reply may take a few days.

Helpful details to include

  • The app name (Deskside Deck, Tenokine, etc.)
  • Your device model and OS version (iOS / iPadOS / watchOS; on iPhone and iPad: Settings > General > About)
  • What you did and what happened (a screenshot helps)

About the information we receive

  • What we receive: your email address, your name (if included), your message, attachments, and your device model and OS version.
  • Purpose: only to reply, investigate issues, and consider your requests to improve our apps.
  • Sharing: we do not share it with anyone except as required by law. We receive and store email with Gmail (Google LLC), which may be stored on servers outside Japan.
  • Retention: we delete it about 2 years after our conversation ends. To have it deleted sooner, email us.
  • On request, we will promptly tell you our name and address, how to request access, correction or deletion, and how we keep your data secure. Complaints can be sent to the same address.
  • Before attaching screenshots, please check they do not show other people’s information or anything you’d rather not share.
